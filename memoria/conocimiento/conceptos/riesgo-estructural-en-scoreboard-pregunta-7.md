En Scoreboard (marcador), el riesgo estructural ocurre cuando no hay unidad funcional libre para la instrucción (opción a). Las estaciones de reserva son de Tomasulo, no de Scoreboard. La opción c describe riesgo de datos (RAW).

> In Scoreboard, a structural risk appears in an arithmetic instruction when: a) There is no functional unit available for its execution b) All reservation stations are busy c) One of the operands of the instruction is still being computed by another arithmetic instruction d) None of the above
